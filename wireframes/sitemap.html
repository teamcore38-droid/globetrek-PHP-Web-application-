Task 01 Deliverable: Hierarchical Sitemap with Role-Based Separation

All Mockups Hub View Live App
Design Rationale
Hierarchical 3-Tier Structure: The sitemap adopts a clear separation of concerns across 3 core user personas (Public/Travelers, Agency Staff, and Administrators). Flat navigation for discovery (tours, custom trips, about, contact) reduces friction and click-depth to 1 click from home. Restricted portals (Staff Operations Desk and Admin Executive Panel) maintain strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to ensure enterprise data security.
